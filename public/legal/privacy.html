Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date] · Effective: [date]

Your trust is the whole point of Phoenix, so we keep this simple: we collect only what we need to run the platform, we never sell your data, and you stay in control of it. Here's exactly what that means.

What we never do
  • We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't run ads, and we don't let advertisers target you.
  • We never see or store your card details — Stripe handles payment.
  • We don't email you unless you asked us to, and you can stop it in one click.

1. What we collect

We collect only what Phoenix needs to work:

  • When you sign up: your name, handle, email, and a password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • When you publish: your work, cover, description, and the choices you make about it.
  • When you buy: a record of what you bought, so you can re-download it.
  • As you use the site: basic technical information like your device type and general activity, to keep things working and secure.

2. Why we collect it

To give you an account, show your work to readers, process sales, pay writers, let people follow and comment, keep the platform safe, and answer you when you reach out. That's it — we don't gather data for purposes we haven't told you about.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you buy or set up payouts, your card or bank details go straight to Stripe — Phoenix never sees or stores them. We keep only a record of the transaction (what was bought, when, and how much) so we can show your purchases and earnings. Stripe has its own privacy policy that covers how it handles your payment information.

4. What's public

Some things are public by design: your handle, your profile and bio if you make it public, the works you publish, and comments you post. Pure readers who haven't published can keep their profile private. Your email, password, purchase history, and payout details are always private.

5. Email and newsletter

We email you about your account (things like a sale, a follow, or a password change) and, only if you opted in, our newsletter. Every newsletter has a working one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribing is honored promptly. You can change your email preferences any time in your settings.

6. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies that are necessary to keep you logged in and the site functioning. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Where the law requires it, we'll ask your consent for anything beyond the strictly necessary — and default to the most private option. [Cookie specifics depend on the analytics and tools Phoenix ends up using — finalise before launch.]

7. Who we share with

We share personal data only with the services that make Phoenix run — for example Stripe for payments and our hosting and email providers — and only as much as each needs to do its job. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people on the platform. We never sell your data.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you close your account, we remove your profile and published work, though we may keep limited records we're legally required to (for example, transaction records for tax). Readers keep copies of works they already bought.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data we hold about you, correct it, download it, or ask us to delete it. You can do much of this directly in your settings; for anything else, contact us and we'll help. We won't charge you for reasonable requests, and we won't penalise you for making one.

10. Children

Phoenix is for adults — you must be 18 or older to hold an account. We don't knowingly collect data from children. Works marked for younger audiences describe who the writing is for; they don't mean children hold accounts. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

11. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures: encrypted connections, securely hashed passwords, and access limited to what's needed to run the platform. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously and will tell you promptly if something affecting your data goes wrong. If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll let members know and update the date above. For any privacy question or request, reach us at [contact email]. To report something that shouldn't be on Phoenix, use the report form.
